Retention Playbook

Acknowledgements

Retention Playbook is an ASTUDIO plugin for turning a submitted customer brief into a reviewed retention plan covering onboarding, churn prevention, payment recovery, win-back, and referrals. It writes and checks text only; it does not send, publish, or access accounts.

Its retention guidance is adapted from the churn-prevention, onboarding-cro and referral-program skills in Corey Haines' marketingskills, licensed under the MIT License. This attribution does not imply endorsement.
