Privacy

Effective 2026-10-11.

Your intentionally submitted brief, plan and tasks are processed in request memory to provide planning output. The application has no user accounts or application database and does not intentionally retain brief or export content.

What the builder does not do

  • No marketing-account connections, tracking, cookies, browser storage, fingerprinting, analytics or advertising pixels.
  • No scraping, live competitor research or outbound network access during tool execution.
  • No training on submitted data.

Operational logs

Application logs contain only tool name, success or error category, latency, aggregate request and response sizes, and validation error code. They do not contain request bodies, brief content, plan content, task content, exports, names, IP addresses or URL query strings. Application logs must be deleted within 14 days.

Hosting and edge providers may keep separate security logs under their published policies. Their actual retention must be confirmed and disclosed before public release.

Your choices

Avoid entering secrets, personal customer data, credentials, customer lists or confidential material. Export files are delivered by the host and are not saved by this service.
