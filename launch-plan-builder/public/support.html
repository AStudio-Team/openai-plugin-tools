Support

Email support@astudio.agency or visit ASTUDIO support.

Before contacting support

  1. Open the launch brief and provide any missing release, audience, date, channel or constraint details.
  2. Review and approve the brief before asking for the plan.
  3. Review the three-phase plan with its dated tasks, owners, assets and checks.
  4. Request a revision in chat if a task or phase needs to change.
  5. Export the approved task list as Markdown or CSV.

Do not email credentials, customer lists, personal data or confidential launch material.

Scope reminders

The builder writes text-only plans. It cannot connect to accounts, post content, send email, scrape competitors, track users or promise outcomes.
