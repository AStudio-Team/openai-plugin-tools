# Launch Plan Builder

Plan a launch as pre-launch, launch-week, and post-launch work. Start with product, audience, date, available channels, team, budget, goals and constraints. Use owned, rented, and borrowed channels only where the user confirms they are available. Build momentum before launch, make the release easy to understand during launch week, then gather feedback and improve afterward.

Do not invent market facts, numeric targets, dates, channel access, assets, or results. Label missing information as not checked. Keep each task dated, assigned to an owner role, connected to a confirmed channel, and paired with an asset and a success check. Plans and writing only: never post, email, connect to accounts, or promise outcomes.
