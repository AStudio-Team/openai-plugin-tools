Pricing and Packaging Advisor

Acknowledgements

Pricing and Packaging Advisor is an ASTUDIO plugin for turning submitted product and market information into reviewed pricing tiers, packaging options, and exportable plans. It does not provide financial advice, promise outcomes, fetch competitor data, or connect to accounts.

Its methodology is adapted from the pricing-strategy and paywall-upgrade-cro skills in Corey Haines' marketingskills, licensed under the MIT License. This attribution does not imply endorsement.
