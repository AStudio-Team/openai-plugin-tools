Privacy policy

This policy explains how Grounded Citations handles sources and draft checks.

Controller

The publisher and data controller is ARAMAN ADVERTISING DMCC, trading as ASTUDIO, 403, 1 Lake Plaza, Cluster T, JLT, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

Data processed

The service receives only the public URLs, temporary file download URLs, file metadata, pasted source text, quotes, draft text, and settings explicitly sent to its MCP tools. It extracts text to build the requested session ledger. It does not request the full conversation, use source content for training, or sell personal data.

Purpose and retention

Extracted text, ledger entries, and verified quote spans stay in bounded volatile memory. They expire after 2 hours without activity or 24 hours after creation, whichever occurs first. The end_session tool deletes active session content immediately. No backups of extracted source content are created.

Operational logs may be retained with container operations for up to 30 days. Logs contain event category, status, latency, aggregate byte counts, source counts, and error class. They exclude source and draft text, quotes, URLs, query strings, filenames, session tokens, and temporary file links.

Hosting, subprocessors, and regions

The service uses cloud hosting, content-delivery, network-security, and the ChatGPT or Codex platform selected by the user. Those providers may process connection metadata in the United Arab Emirates, Europe, the United States, or another region where they operate. ASTUDIO uses provider contracts and appropriate safeguards for cross-border processing where required. OpenAI and any host platform process conversations and files under their own terms.

Security and user control

Remote retrieval is limited to public HTTPS destinations and applies DNS, redirect, size, time, MIME, extraction, and rate limits. You decide which sources to provide, review scope before ledger creation, and may delete the session at any time.

Requests

Use the support page for privacy questions or support-record deletion. Never send passwords, temporary source links, session tokens, or confidential source text through the support form.
