Ad Creative Lab

Build a structured campaign brief, approve it at a signed human checkpoint, compare 3 to 5 text-only concepts, select or request revisions, validate current platform copy limits, and export CSV, JSON or Markdown. What it does

  • Your approval before any concept

  • Character-limit checks per platform
  • Export as CSV, JSON or Markdown

Ask ChatGPT

  • “Check this ad copy against Meta and LinkedIn length limits.”
