Privacy policy

This policy explains how ASTUDIO handles information when you use any of its twenty ChatGPT plugins: PDF Table to Excel, GCC Arabic Writer, Ad Creative Lab, Growth Marketing Planner, AI Crawler Readiness Auditor, Editable Diagram Builder, Open Data Fetcher, Grounded Citations, Verified Buy Link, Launch Plan Builder, Landing Page Copy Check, Schema Markup Generator, Social Content Planner, Cold Email Writer, Comparison Page Builder, Sales Collateral Builder, Pricing and Packaging Advisor, Content Strategy Planner, A/B Test Planner and Retention Playbook.

Controller

The service publisher and data controller is ARAMAN ADVERTISING DMCC, trading as ASTUDIO, 403, 1 Lake Plaza, Cluster T, JLT, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

Data we collect

We collect only what you type, select or upload into a plugin's tools in ChatGPT. We also keep minimal technical request logs: the time, the tool name, the result status and any error text. Brief content is deliberately not written to these logs. Our hosting and network providers may process basic connection data, such as IP address and request timing, to deliver and secure the service.

Why we use it (purposes)

We use your input only to produce the result you asked for in that conversation. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, sale or model training. Logs are used only to keep the service running and secure.

Who receives it (recipients)

The recipients are: ASTUDIO as publisher; our hosting provider as processor; and ChatGPT (OpenAI) as the host that passes your tool inputs to the plugin and shows you the results. The following plugins also fetch public sources, described in their blocks below: AI Crawler Readiness Auditor, Open Data Fetcher, Grounded Citations and Verified Buy Link. No one else receives your data.

How long we keep it (retention)

Retention is stated for each plugin below. Nothing is written to a database, except the temporary store named for Open Data Fetcher. Generated workbooks from PDF Table to Excel are held for no more than 15 minutes. A periodic expiration process removes them even when no later request occurs. Container logs are size-limited and rotated, but no fixed time-based deletion period is promised. Logs disappear when a retired service container is removed.

Your controls

  • You choose what to provide, and you can stop using a plugin or disconnect it in ChatGPT at any time.
  • You can ask for access to or deletion of anything linked to you through the support page: https://www.astudio.agency/tools/support
  • Because content is not stored after the response, there is normally nothing to delete.
  • Grounded Citations has an end-session tool that clears its session at once.

What each plugin receives and returns

PDF Table to Excel

Receives: the PDF you select, its temporary download URL, basic file details and your output filename.
Returns: a workbook link, the filename, counts, worksheet metadata and up to five sample rows per table.
Kept: the workbook is held for no more than 15 minutes and removed by a periodic expiration process. PDF input is processed in working memory and not intentionally saved. No fixed time-based deletion period is promised beyond that.
Network: downloads only the file URL the host supplies. The result app makes no direct network request; your host mediates opening the download link.

GCC Arabic Writer

Receives: the brief or copy you supply. Its brief app sends reviewed fields into chat only when you press the visible send button, and it does not silently update model context.
Returns: a normalized brief, lint results and fidelity results.
Kept: nothing after the response.
Network: none. It does not call an LLM.

Ad Creative Lab

Receives: your campaign brief, concept choice and copy to check.
Returns: a reviewed brief, concept options, validation, an export package and an approval receipt (a signed code that proves you approved the brief).
Kept: receipts expire on their own; nothing else is kept after the response.
Network: none.

Growth Marketing Planner

Receives: your planning brief, strategy choice and plan text.
Returns: strategies, the final plan and an export.
Kept: nothing after the response.
Network: none.

AI Crawler Readiness Auditor

Receives: one public page address that you confirm.
Returns: an audit report.
Kept: no copy of fetched content after the response.
Network: fetches that page, its robots.txt, sitemap, llms.txt and llms-full.txt from the public internet.

Editable Diagram Builder

Receives: your brief, scene and edits.
Returns: a diagram preview and an editable file.
Kept: in memory only. Checkpoint tokens hold a digest, timestamps and protocol version, and they expire.
Network: none.

Open Data Fetcher

Receives: your search terms, the dataset you choose, and your columns and filters.
Returns: dataset lists, previews and a temporary export file.
Kept: previews are not kept. Only the export file you confirm is held in a temporary in-memory store, for up to 60 minutes, and is then removed.
Network: searches the three catalogs, US Data.gov, UK Data.gov.uk and World Bank Open Data, and, for preview and export, downloads the chosen dataset file from the official government or institution host that the catalog lists, limited to a fixed list of approved hosts: US federal agencies such as CDC, Census and BLS, GOV.UK, London Datastore, and World Bank.

Grounded Citations

Receives: the source pages, documents or pasted text you supply, the quotes to verify and your draft.
Returns: a citation ledger with source names, locations of quotes, access times and a session reference.
Kept: in a volatile in-memory session that you can end at any time and that expires on its own after 2 idle hours or 24 hours in total.
Network: fetches the public source pages you supply (and any redirects they follow) and downloads the documents you attach through the temporary download address the host provides; private and internal addresses are blocked.

Verified Buy Link

Receives: the product details and product page addresses you paste and confirm.
Returns: an evidence comparison.
Kept: the report is held encrypted in memory for up to 30 minutes and then expires.
Network: fetches only the product pages you paste, and the robots.txt of those sites to respect their access rules, and only for retailers on its approved list. It never buys, logs in or adds to a cart.

Security

No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so avoid submitting highly sensitive, regulated or restricted data, customer records or credentials.
