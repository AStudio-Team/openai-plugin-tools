PDF Table

Upload a text-based PDF. Review the filename, pages, detected tables, worksheet previews and expiry, then download through the host or ask to rename, inspect or convert another file.

20 MB maximum

Best for: reports, price lists, schedules, statements and research tables with selectable text.

Not for: scanned image-only PDFs. OCR is not included in this release.

How it works

From attachment to workbook.

Attach a PDF

Choose the PDF in ChatGPT or Codex.

Ask for conversion

Say: Convert every table in this PDF to Excel.

Download the XLSX

The temporary link stays available for 15 minutes.

Separate worksheets

Each detected table gets a clearly named sheet.

Formula-safe cells

Untrusted values are neutralized so extracted text cannot become a spreadsheet formula.

Private by design

The input is processed in memory. The output is held temporarily and expires automatically.
