Research-only MCP App

Bring the links.
Check the evidence.

Verified Buy Link compares one to five exact product pages that you supply. It checks product and variant identity, current purchase controls, price, retailer identity clues, authorization evidence and return restrictions when documented access rights and the site's robots policy permit retrieval.

Human controlled

You confirm the exact product, delivery market and constraints before research. You select the candidate. The app rechecks it before asking the host to open a normalized product page.

It never buys, reserves, logs in, adds to cart, starts checkout or submits a retailer form.

How it works

Bounded by your exact pages

1. Confirm scope

Enter product identity, one to five exact HTTPS product pages, delivery country, currency, budget, conditions and exclusions.

2. Compare evidence

Pages are inspected only when a current source-rights record and robots policy permit it. Unsupported sources fail closed.

3. Select and recheck

No candidate opens automatically. A selected page is rechecked immediately, then opened through the MCP host only if the link remains eligible.

What a verdict means

Go means the supplied page had strong evidence for exact identity, current purchase control, retailer identity and constraints. Conditional go keeps material gaps prominent. No-go marks a conflict or unacceptable result. Unable to verify means access or evidence was unavailable.

Every verdict is an evidence-based research assessment, not a guarantee of retailer conduct, product authenticity, inventory, delivery or transaction safety. A barcode match establishes only an identity signal, not retailer legitimacy, brand authorization or authenticity.

No retailer discovery

Version 1 does not search the web for sellers, scrape arbitrary retailers, use unlicensed review data or accept paid placement. It processes only the exact product-page URLs supplied in the confirmed checkpoint.
