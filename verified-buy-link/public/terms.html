Terms of service

Verified Buy Link provides bounded research about exact public product pages supplied by the user. It is not a seller, marketplace, broker or purchasing agent.

Research boundary

The service does not purchase, reserve, add to cart, start checkout, log in, create accounts, submit retailer forms, send messages, upload files or collect payment or address data. It does not discover retailers or search for offers. It may decline to inspect any source.

No guarantees

Results are evidence-based assessments, not guarantees of authenticity, legality, safety, inventory, pricing, delivery, returns or retailer conduct. Evidence can change after retrieval. Verify the page, seller, price, variant and terms before transacting.

Source access

You may supply only public product-detail URLs that you are entitled to share. Supplying a URL does not establish permission for automated retrieval. The service separately requires a current source-rights record and robots permission. It does not bypass login walls, CAPTCHA, rate limits or technical restrictions.

External pages

An external product page opens only after deliberate human selection and an immediate recheck, using the MCP host's link control. The retailer controls all later interactions. ASTUDIO is not responsible for third-party content, terms or transactions.

Acceptable use

Do not submit private-network locations, credentials, payment data, addresses, illegal content, shortened transaction links or URLs intended to probe internal systems. Do not use the service to evade a site's rules or automate purchasing.

Availability

The service may rate-limit, reject or stop a request for legal, security, reliability or source-policy reasons. Reports expire after 30 minutes.
