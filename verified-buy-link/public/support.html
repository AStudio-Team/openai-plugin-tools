The page was not fetched

This usually means the domain lacks a current source-rights record, robots rules disallow the path, the destination failed network safety checks, or the page requires login or CAPTCHA. The service shows the user-supplied URL as unverified but does not produce a Go verdict.

The result says authorization not proven

Version 1 inspects only exact supplied product pages. A retailer page normally cannot prove independent target-brand authorization. The app keeps that limitation visible rather than treating another brand's badge or a barcode as proof.

The price or purchase control changed

Select Recheck now. Open product page also performs an immediate recheck. If the exact variant or purchase control cannot be verified, the app withdraws the open action.
