Privacy policy

Verified Buy Link is anonymous and account-free. It receives only the product research scope that you explicitly confirm.

Controller

ARAMAN ADVERTISING DMCC, trading as ASTUDIO, 403, 1 Lake Plaza, Cluster T, JLT, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

Data processed

The service processes product identity and variant details, one to five exact public product-page URLs, commercial delivery country, currency, budget, permitted conditions, timing, marketplace and return preferences, excluded domains and your candidate selection.

It does not request or need your name, email, phone number, street address, city, GPS location, payment details, retailer credentials, files, contacts, advertising identifier or chat history.

Purpose and recipients

The service uses the confirmed scope only to retrieve the exact public pages you supplied and return typed evidence. A controlled HTTPS client connects directly to a permitted source domain. No search, review or advertising provider receives your product query. Your MCP host processes the tool input and result under its own privacy terms.

Retention

Research reports are encrypted in volatile application memory and expire no more than 30 minutes after creation. Rechecking does not extend that expiry. Raw fetched page content is parsed in memory and is not written to application storage. An ephemeral HMAC abuse bucket keeps only an unlinkable digest and counters for no more than 15 minutes. Application event logs contain action names and status only, not URLs, product text, prompts or raw IP addresses. Infrastructure security logs may be retained for up to 30 days.

Automated source access

A source is fetched only after a current source-rights record confirms a reviewed access basis and its robots policy permits the exact path. Missing or restrictive records stop retrieval. See the source-rights model.

Deletion and contact

Reports expire automatically. Because the service has no accounts and does not retain a user identifier, it cannot look up a report after expiry. For privacy questions, use the support page.
