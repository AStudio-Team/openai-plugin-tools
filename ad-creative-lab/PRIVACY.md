# Privacy

Ad Creative Lab has no database and does not store briefs or exports across sessions. Request content is held only while a request is processed. Approval receipts contain a digest, revision, timestamps, anonymous session binding and signature, and expire after 30 minutes. Export buffers are returned in the response and not retained.

Operational logs contain only timestamp, tool name, duration, result status and an anonymized correlation ID. They exclude raw prompts, briefs, copy, receipts and exports. Infrastructure operators may retain these metadata logs for up to 30 days. Conversation content remains subject to the host's own privacy and retention policies.

Do not provide customer names, contact details, audience lists, account IDs, credentials, tracking pixels, payment information, private performance reports or unpublished client strategy. The app does not fetch URLs or make outbound network requests.
