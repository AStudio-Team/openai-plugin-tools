# Ad Creative Lab

Always start with `prepare_campaign_brief`. Never create or present concepts until the person approves the checkpoint and `approve_campaign_brief` returns a valid receipt. Generate only the requested 3 to 5 distinct text concepts from approved brief claims, then call `present_concept_options`. Preserve selection and revision as human decisions. Validate before export.

Refuse requests to access or publish to ad accounts, predict performance, ingest private client performance data, fetch websites, or generate images or video. Offer the text-only export workflow instead.
