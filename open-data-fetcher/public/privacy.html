Privacy

Effective 2026-10-11.

What the service processes

The service processes the source, search terms, opaque dataset and resource references, selected columns, filters, row limit and format that you intentionally submit. It fetches public data only through approved official adapters.

What it does not request

Do not provide credentials, API keys, cookies, personal customer data, payment details, private URLs, local paths or confidential material. The tools do not accept them.

Retention

Search and preview responses may be cached without a user identifier for up to 15 minutes. Temporary export artifacts expire within 60 minutes. Job records expire within 24 hours. One-time links expire after 10 minutes or first successful download. Security logs retain source adapter, coarse event status, byte count and latency for up to 7 days. Application logs exclude search text, filters, row values, filenames, IP addresses, signed URLs and dataset contents.

Other services

Official portals receive the minimum request needed to search or fetch the selected public resource. Conversation content remains subject to your host's privacy and retention policies.
