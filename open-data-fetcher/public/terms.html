Service scope

Open Data Fetcher helps you search approved official public catalogs, inspect metadata, preview bounded samples and create temporary exports when the recorded rights status is green. It does not provide legal advice or certify that your downstream use is lawful.

Your responsibilities

You must review the publisher's current license, attribution requirements, source notice and applicable terms. Do not use the service to access private, credentialed, restricted or unauthorized material.

Rights safeguards

Green permits server export with attribution. Amber sends you to the publisher by default. Red blocks server preview and export. These controls are conservative operational decisions, not legal conclusions.

Availability

Official sources can change, fail, rate-limit requests or correct their data. Metadata, size estimates and samples may be incomplete. Verify material decisions against the publisher's current page.

Prohibited use

Do not attempt SSRF, local-network access, credential forwarding, access-control bypass, archive bombs, malicious files, service abuse or infringement of third-party rights.
